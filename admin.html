Site settings

Photos, logo and colours. Changes show on the live site right after you save.

Choose a photo, click the preview to set the focus point, then save.

Logo tips. Upload a PNG, WebP or SVG with a transparent background. The site picks the right version on its own: light background = a dark logo (shown on the white top bar and light sections), dark background = a light logo (shown on the black top bar, the photo and the footer). If you upload only one, it is used everywhere, so make sure it is readable on both. Emblem is the symbol, wordmark is the name written out.
Colors. Light mode and dark mode have their own set, because visitors see the one that matches their device. Pick a preset or fine-tune each colour. The small preview updates as you go; nothing changes on the live site until you press Save colors.
