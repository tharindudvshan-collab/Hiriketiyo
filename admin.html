All changes published
Edit any words on the site. Type in a box and the preview updates at once. Or press Click to edit above the preview and click any text on the page. Switch sections on or off with the Show boxes. Nothing goes live until you press Publish changes.
Choose a photo, click the picture to set the focus point, then press Save image. The preview shows your choice before you save. Photos are saved to the live site straight away; empty slots are skipped by the slideshows.
Logo tips. Upload a PNG, WebP or SVG with a transparent background. Light background = a dark logo (white top bar and light sections). Dark background = a light logo (black top bar, the photo and the footer). If you upload only one, it is used everywhere. Emblem is the symbol, wordmark is the name written out.
Design. Start with a Quick style, then fine-tune. Light mode and dark mode have their own colors, because visitors see the one that matches their device. Use Auto / Light / Dark above the preview to check both.

Quick styles

Products for the Shopping page. Add a product with its name, price (LKR), description and up to 3 photos, then press Save product. It goes live straight away (no need to press Publish). Switch Show on the website off to hide a product without deleting it. Use ↑ ↓ to change the order.
Payment options. Choose how customers can pay for the products you add here. They pick one in the product window, and the choice is included in their order message. Only ticked options are shown. Do not type account numbers here unless you are happy for every visitor to see them. Partner products that already use the secure card checkout are not affected.

Custom CSS

For designers. Extra style rules applied on top of everything else, visible in the preview as you type. Be careful: a mistake here can break the layout. Clear the box to remove it.

Backup & restore

Download your text, links, switches and design as one file, or load a file you saved earlier. Photos are stored separately and are not in the backup.

Start over

Puts every edited word, link and Show switch back to what is written in index.html. Colors and photos are not touched.
